# Attentive — Outcome Research

## What Attentive Promises
Attentive is an AI-powered SMS and email marketing platform that turns text messages into a measurable, high-ROI revenue channel for ecommerce brands.

## The Transformation (Before → After)
- **Before**: Brands blast generic emails, get 1-2% click rates, have no direct mobile channel to customers. SMS is unused or poorly executed with no personalization.
- **After**: Brands have a direct, personalized SMS+email channel that generates 20-30% of total online revenue. Messages are AI-optimized, sent at perfect timing, with personalized product recommendations. Subscriber lists grow 2x faster with two-tap technology.

## Key Metrics That Prove It Works
- **$500M+ ARR** for Attentive itself (proves brands pay serious money for this)
- **8,000+ brands** using the platform
- **$1.2B in recovered abandoned cart revenue** across all clients
- **Cart abandonment SMS**: 33% CTR, 19% CVR, $8.00 revenue per send
- **Browse abandonment SMS**: 21% CTR, 2.5% CVR, $2.60 revenue per send
- **875M+ SMS opt-ins** collected through sign-up units
- **590M+ email opt-ins** collected
- **5.7B messages** sent during Cyber Week 2025 alone

## Why Users Pay
1. **Direct revenue attribution** — Every SMS/email is tracked to purchases. Brands see exact ROI.
2. **Subscriber growth** — Two-tap technology makes it frictionless to build SMS lists (2x growth rate).
3. **Automation** — Set up journeys once (welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase) and they run forever generating revenue.
4. **AI optimization** — AI picks the best send time, content, and audience automatically.
5. **Compliance built-in** — TCPA compliance is complex and risky. Attentive handles it.

## Pain Points Solved
- SMS compliance is a legal minefield (TCPA lawsuits) → Attentive handles all compliance
- Building SMS subscriber lists is hard → Two-tap makes it effortless
- Personalizing at scale is impossible manually → AI automates personalization
- Proving SMS ROI is difficult → Revenue attribution built into every message
- Managing campaigns across SMS + email is fragmented → Unified platform

## Target Customer
- eCommerce brands doing $100K+/month in revenue
- Already have email marketing generating 25%+ of revenue
- Dedicated marketing staff
- View SMS as a strategic priority, not a side channel
